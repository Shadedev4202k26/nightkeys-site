Privacy Policy

Nightkeys · Last updated 28 August 2026

Nightkeys collects no data of any kind. Not anonymised data, not analytics, not crash reports, not usage statistics.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, transmit, or store personal information.
  • It does not connect to the internet. It has no network capability at all.
  • It contains no analytics, telemetry, tracking, or advertising software.
  • It shares nothing with any third party, because it has nothing to share.
  • It does not access your files, camera, microphone, location, or contacts.
  • It does not read your keystrokes. Despite the name, Nightkeys has no access to your keyboard input — it only draws light on your screen.

What is stored on your Mac

Your preferences — brightness, colour temperature, hue, saturation, bar height, and soft edge — are saved locally on your own computer using the standard macOS preferences system. They never leave your Mac. Deleting the app removes them.

Permissions

Nightkeys runs in Apple's App Sandbox and requests no entitlements or permissions whatsoever. macOS will never prompt you to grant it access to anything, because it never asks for anything.

Purchases

Nightkeys is sold through the Mac App Store. Any payment, refund, or subscription information is handled entirely by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. I receive only aggregate sales totals from Apple, which contain no information identifying individual customers.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. If a future version of the app collects anything at all, that change will be stated here clearly and in advance.
